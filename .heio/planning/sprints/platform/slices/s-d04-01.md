# D04.01 Cross-compile: at least one non-host triple smoke

## Why

ROADMAP D04.01 is the non-host triple LLVM smoke on the distribution location. Parent D04 names the available linux/darwin/windows × amd64/arm64 compile surface and does not require a non-host success. This cut proves the LLVM backend can emit for at least one triple that is not the host.
