## Product design principles

1. **Calm over flashy** - predictable, trustworthy
2. **Functional clarity** - hierarchy over decoration
3. **Consistent tokens only** - no one-off palettes
4. **Progressive disclosure** - show what the task needs now. Reveal the rest on demand
5. **Responsive by default** - touch targets ≥44px. Check 375 / 768 / 1440

**Incorrect:** animated gradients, dense icon rows, and every filter visible on first paint.

**Correct:** clear primary action, readable structure, secondary controls behind an obvious next step.

**Notes.** When two options look equal, pick the calmer one. Delight comes from low friction, not ornament.
