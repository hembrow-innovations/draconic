## Avoid decorative complexity

Skip ornament that does not help the user finish the job. Extra shadows, gradients, badge piles, and motion compete with content.

**Incorrect:** glassmorphism cards, continuous ambient animation, icon-only rows with no labels for primary tasks.

**Correct:** clear structure, one primary action, labels on destructive or irreversible controls.

**Notes.** If removing the decoration does not hurt comprehension, remove it.
