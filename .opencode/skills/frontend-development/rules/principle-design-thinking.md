## Design thinking before chrome

Before choosing layout or decoration, answer four questions. Skip them and you ship generic dashboard chrome.

- **Purpose.** What problem does this screen solve for the person using it?
- **Context.** Which domain and constraints shape the data?
- **Tone.** Calm and functional. Reduce mental load.
- **Visibility.** Private, shared, or restricted states when the screen involves owned data.

**Incorrect:** starting from a flashy card grid, then forcing domain data into it.

**Correct:** name the user job and domain constraints, then pick the simplest hierarchy that answers the job (list, form, detail, status).

**Notes.** Ownership and sharing are product facts. Surface private vs shared when the screen involves shared data. Do not hide ownership.
