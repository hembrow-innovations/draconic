# Purpose is job and fences

`purpose.md` is the product brief for one area. Copy **docs** `templates/purpose.md`. One per area. Nested purpose only when that folder has its own job.

## Job

One short statement of why the area exists. Product outcome only.

## In scope

What this area is allowed to do.

## Out of scope

Hard fences. The things an agent will invent if you leave them unsaid.

## Surfaces

Where a user meets this area.

## Authority

Wikilink the contracts, test.md notes, and binding ADRs. Do not paste promises here.

## Open product questions

Unresolved product direction. `(none)` means an agent may proceed. A live question means stop.

Purpose does not hold how, file trees, framework choice, or React Query. That is data-flows or architecture. Behaviour lives in `contract.md`. Coverage lives in `test.md`.
