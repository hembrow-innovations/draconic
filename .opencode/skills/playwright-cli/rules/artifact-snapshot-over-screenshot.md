## Snapshot to decide. Screenshot to prove

The accessibility snapshot is how you choose the next ref. A PNG is for a human or for proof.

**Incorrect:** Taking a screenshot after every click and guessing coordinates from the image.

**Correct:** `snapshot` (or `find`) to act. `screenshot` / `pdf` when the user needs to see the page, or when you must attach proof.

Notes: `show --annotate` is the human review path (`inspect-console-requests`).
